About

A scholar's rigour, a conversationalist's warmth

I'm Demyan Petrov, a language tutor devoted to helping students move beyond textbooks and into fluent, natural communication. My teaching blends academic structure with the everyday rhythm of real speech — because grammar matters, but so does feeling at home in a language.

My philosophy is simple: every learner is different, so every lesson is built around you — your goals, your pace, and the moments where the language finally clicks.

Beyond teaching Spanish and English to Russian speakers, I also welcome international students who want to learn Russian as a foreign language — guiding them from the Cyrillic alphabet to confident everyday conversation.

  • ◆M.A. in Applied Linguistics — specialisation in second-language acquisition.
  • ◆Certified examiner for DELE (Spanish) and Cambridge / IELTS (English).
  • ◆10+ years of one-to-one tutoring, online and in person.
  • ◆Trilingual fluency in Russian, Spanish and English, refined by years abroad.
